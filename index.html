🍄 OYSTER MUSHROOM FARMING — COMPLETE PROCESS

From spawn to harvest and market — every stage is organized for professional farm management.

1

Quality Spawn

Select clean, vigorous oyster mushroom spawn and verify quality.

2

Substrate Preparation

Prepare and condition the growing substrate using clean procedures.

3

Bag Filling

Fill growing bags consistently and maintain clean working conditions.

4

Inoculation

Introduce spawn into prepared bags while minimizing contamination.

5

Incubation

Maintain a suitable clean environment while mycelium colonizes.

6

Fruiting

Manage humidity, ventilation and hygiene for fruiting.

7

Harvest

Harvest mature oyster clusters and record weight and batch data.

8

Sorting & Packing

Grade, clean and pack mushrooms professionally.

9

Sales & Delivery

Record customers, prices, transport and delivered quantities.

10

Reinvestment

Track results and reinvest in capacity, equipment and employment.
